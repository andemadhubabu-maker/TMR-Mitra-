    How to use · ఎలా ఉపయోగించాలి · कैसे उपयोग करें

    TMR Mitra – Version 1 Beta. This is a helper that drafts control messages and reports. It is not an official Railway tool and does not replace rule books, Control's orders or the phone call. Always read the message and check it before you send. Everything stays on your phone.

    1. Choose the message

    The bottom bar has three steps: 1 Choose, 2 Fill, 3 Send. In Choose, tap a category tile (19 categories, 139 scenarios), then the scenario. You can also type a word or code in Search all messages (for example rail fracture, G5.5) and tap the result. The language buttons (English, తెలుగు, हिन्दी) change only the titles; messages stay in English. ☆ Pin keeps a scenario you use often at the top. The zone / division chip at the top sets your zone and division once, so station suggestions fit your area.

    2. Quick entry: say or type one line

    In Choose, tap 🎤 and speak, or type one sentence, for example "chain pulled 12841 km 527/17 mid section coach S3", then tap Fill. The app picks the scenario and fills only what you said. Anything not said stays blank; it never guesses. Check every box. Voice needs a phone with speech typing and, on many phones, internet.

    3. Fill in the details

    • Text boxes: type time (24-hour, for example 14:25), train number, KM, coach number and so on. The 🎤 beside a box lets you speak into it. Now puts the current time.
    • Where did the train stop? Choose mid-section, station or platform. Only the boxes that fit are shown.
    • Dropdowns and Reason: choose one. If your reason is not listed, choose Other (type manually) and type it.
    • Informed to: tap and tick one or more (Security, Commercial, Punctuality, C&W, TXR Control and more). Tick Other to type a name. Tick DYSS / Station name and type the station.
    • More details (optional): extra boxes. Leave them empty and the sentence is left out.
    • Detention: filled automatically when Time and Left At are filled. You can type over it.
    • TMR No.: comes from My profile (your Phone number, as in Control messages).
    • A box that is needed but empty shows as ___ in the message. A box that is hidden for your choice is ignored; a small note lists such values.

    Load example fills sample details so you can see how it works. Reset (clear form) clears the form and the evidence.

    4. Preview, Short or Full, and editing

    In Send you see the Live preview. Choose Full or Short wording. Read it from start to end. ✎ Edit message text lets you change the words by hand; Use generated text again brings the original back. The strip above the bottom bar also shows a small preview with Copy and Send →.

    5. Recheck before sending

    Before Copy or Send, a Recheck before sending window shows blank parts and “please check” warnings (for example a train number that looks wrong or times that do not fit). Tap Go back and fill to correct, or Send anyway if you are sure.

    6. Name and phone at the end of the message

    Tap 👤 My profile. Fill Name, Designation, Phone number and CMS ID / TMR No. (the ID only; the phone goes in its own box). Tick Add my name and phone number at the end of every message and tap Save profile. Messages then end like “TMR: Name, Ph: 98XXXXXXXX.” If the phone box is empty the message shows Ph: ___. If you typed the phone in the CMS ID box, it is moved to the Phone box when you save. The profile is also used in the PDF, the signature pad and Reports. A− and A+ (bigger or smaller text) are in the same place.

    7. Evidence: photo, video, audio

    • A photo or video authenticates our work. Attach it whenever possible. It stays on your phone.
    • Take photo, Record video, Add from gallery, 📷 Multi camera (several shots one after another). An optional stamp can add date, time, train and km.
    • 📋 Photos to take shows a shot list for the scenario.
    • 🎙 Audio Note records up to 60 seconds. Tap × or Clear all to remove.
    • The message then ends with “Evidence: … attached.” Evidence clears when you change the scenario or tap Reset.

    8. Copy, send and save

    • Copy message – then paste it in WhatsApp or SMS.
    • Send message with evidence – the phone's share list opens; choose WhatsApp and the group. Check that the text came with the files. If sharing is not supported, copy and paste.
    • Send message only – opens WhatsApp or the share list with the text ready.
    • Download PDF Report – saves the message as a PDF. ✍ Sign pad adds your signature.
    • Save for later – keeps the message on the phone (useful with no signal). Photos are not saved.
    • ➕ Follow-up message (keep train details) – opens another scenario with train, loco, section and station already filled.
    • Follow-up: Train Ready / Started (last category) – the second message to Control after the train is attended and made fit: ready time, left time (or none), and what it waited for (line clear, crossing, precedence, path). Also a version for the next station: “On arrival at ___ … attended and reset”.
    • Stopped again at other places: in Pressure Drop, Brake Binding and Flat Tyre, tap ➕ Add stop for each place and the minutes lost there. The total detention is added up automatically (“Total det +13 mins”).
    • Where did it stop? in most stopping scenarios, this comes right after the train and time: mid-section, passing through a station, arriving / standing / starting at a platform (scheduled or non-scheduled stop, with PF no.), or in the station yard. The boxes underneath change with your choice and the message wording follows it.
    • Informed to: in ACP, C&W, OHE, engineering, medical and security messages the usual offices are pre-selected (for example ACP: Security, TXR and Commercial Control). Check them and tap to add or remove any.
    • ⚠ Report wrong wording in this message – opens the Share list with the message and a blank for what is wrong, ready to send to the person who looks after the app.

    9. Saved messages and Reuse

    Saved messages lists what you saved. ♻ Reuse a recent message keeps the last 20 messages you copied, sent or saved. ♻ Reuse opens the same scenario with every detail filled; change only what is different. Reuse, clear times also clears the stop time, left time and detention. Messages saved before this update cannot be reused. Photos are not kept. Clear this list removes only the recent list.

    10. 🚨 Emergency window

    Tap 🚨 Emergency at the top. Speak or type what happened, train number and KM (🎤 Speak, 🎤 Add words), then send with WhatsApp, SMS or Copy to your saved contacts. Then open the full message ▸ carries on in the normal screens. Add contacts under Quick send contacts.

    11. 🛠 Troubleshooting checklists and timers

    Tap 🛠 Troubleshoot or Open the checklist for this message. Cards come from the LHB Made Easy notes and the SCR Accident Manual (ACP, fire alarm, brake and other cases). Tick each step to time-stamp it, then Copy log. Time since stop and detention gives a stop timer and a detention calculator. You can add your own checklist. Always follow the current rules and Control's orders first.

    12. Reports

    📄 Prepare report from this message or Reports opens the report maker: TMR report, Loco Pilot report and Joint report. Add the signature on the pad, then save as PDF or send as WhatsApp text with proof files. Saved reports lists old reports.

    13. 📋 My record

    In ⋯ More → My record keep your PME and RC / First Aid dates, Automatic signal certificate, First aid box, detonators and promotion dates. The app works out the next due date and warns on the home screen: 1 month before for PME and RC, 1 week before for the Automatic signal certificate and First aid box (detonators 30 days). Nothing is sent anywhere.

    14. 📚 My Library and Rule book amendments

    In ⋯ More → My Library keep ROM, SOB, Safety circulars, Flyleaf pages, SOP, Ready reckoner and your own types. Add a card by typing, speaking, photo, gallery or PDF. Cards are newest first (or A–Z), show a photo and page count, and can have a valid-until date. Inside a card use A−/A+, Copy text, Edit, optional ☆ Pin, Share, Export card and Delete (Undo for 9 seconds). Nothing shows on the home screen unless you pin it.

    The second tab, Rule book amendments, is for General Rules (G&SR), Subsidiary Rules and the Accident Manual:

    • ＋ Add amendment – AS number, date, a note and a photo or PDF of the slip. Save and add next opens the next number.
    • ＋ Add several – “From AS 11 To AS 23” makes one entry for each number (up to 60). Tap an entry later to add its date or photo.
    • The tab shows the latest AS and a yellow note for AS numbers not entered in between. The same AS cannot be entered twice in a book.

    15. ⚙ Station codes, contacts and backup

    • Extra station codes, My trains, and Import stations and trains (CSV or JSON) improve suggestions.
    • Quick send contacts are used by the Emergency window. Recent entries can be cleared.
    • Depot starter pack: a senior TMR taps Make starter pack and sends the file; a colleague taps Use starter pack. It has no name, phone, signature, messages or Library.
    • Check this install → Check now shows whether the app is installed, works offline and how much storage is left. Ask phone to keep my data asks the phone not to clear it.
    • Backup: use Download backup (or Backup now in My profile) at least once a month. A reminder appears every 30 days. Restore from backup brings your data back after a new phone or a clear-up. The Library has its own Export and Import.

    16. Other buttons

    • 🌙 Night – dark screen. ☀️ Outdoor – strong contrast for sunlight.
    • ✍ Free message – write or speak your own message when no scenario fits.
    • 🎓 Training (in ⋯ More) – for practice. A TRAINING mark shows on the preview so a practice message is not mistaken for a real one.
    • What's new appears once after an update.

    17. Worked example

    Category 1. Alarm Chain Pulling (ACP), scenario A. Tap Load example to see it. The message looks like this:

    At 08:32 hrs, T.no: 12841 exp (HWH–MAS) stopped between BPY – CEL at km: 527/17 due to ACP in coach no: SER LS5A 255168 (LHB), 03rd from T.E. Reason: Theft / mobile theft. Same was attended and isolated by ALP due to heavy crowd in coach. Train ready and left at 08:39 hrs. Det+07 mins. Security Control informed. TMR No: XXXXXXXXXX. TMR: Your Name, Ph: 98XXXXXXXX.

    18. Install and use offline

    Open the link in Chrome on Android, tap ⋮ → Install app (or Add to Home screen); on iPhone use Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Open it once with internet and wait a few seconds. After that it works without internet. A new version loads the next time you open the app with internet; your data stays.

    19. Tips

    • Changing the category or scenario clears what you typed. Finish and copy one message first (or use Follow-up or Reuse).
    • Back up monthly. Clearing browser data or changing phones removes everything that is not backed up.
    • Report wrong wording, bugs and ideas to andemadhubabu@gmail.com or with the Report button.

    नीचे की पट्टी में तीन चरण हैं: 1 Choose, 2 Fill, 3 Send। Choose में श्रेणी की टाइल (19 श्रेणियाँ, 139 सीनारियो) और फिर सीनारियो चुनें। Search all messages में शब्द या कोड (जैसे rail fracture, G5.5) लिखकर नतीजा दबा सकते हैं। भाषा बटन (English, తెలుగు, हिन्दी) केवल शीर्षक बदलते हैं; संदेश English में ही रहते हैं। ☆ Pin बार-बार काम आने वाले सीनारियो को ऊपर रखता है। ऊपर के zone / division चिप में अपना ज़ोन और डिवीज़न एक बार तय करें, ताकि स्टेशन के सुझाव आपके क्षेत्र के हों।

    2. Quick entry: एक पंक्ति बोलें या लिखें

    Choose में 🎤 दबाकर बोलें, या एक वाक्य लिखें, जैसे “chain pulled 12841 km 527/17 mid section coach S3”, फिर Fill दबाएँ। ऐप सीनारियो चुनता है और केवल वही भरता है जो आपने बोला। जो नहीं बोला वह खाली रहता है; ऐप अंदाज़ा नहीं लगाता। हर बॉक्स जाँच लें। आवाज़ के लिए स्पीच टाइपिंग वाला फ़ोन चाहिए और कई फ़ोन में इंटरनेट भी।

    3. विवरण भरें

    • टेक्स्ट बॉक्स: समय (24 घंटे में, जैसे 14:25), ट्रेन नंबर, KM, कोच नंबर आदि लिखें। बॉक्स के पास 🎤 से बोल सकते हैं। Now मौजूदा समय डालता है।
    • ट्रेन कहाँ रुकी? मिड-सेक्शन, स्टेशन या प्लेटफ़ॉर्म चुनें। केवल सही बैठने वाले बॉक्स दिखते हैं।
    • ड्रॉपडाउन और Reason: एक चुनें। कारण सूची में न हो तो Other (type manually) चुनकर लिखें।
    • Informed to: दबाकर एक या अधिक पर टिक करें (Security, Commercial, Punctuality, C&W, TXR Control आदि)। नाम लिखने के लिए Other टिक करें। DYSS / Station name टिक करके स्टेशन लिखें।
    • More details (optional): अतिरिक्त बॉक्स। खाली छोड़ें तो वह वाक्य संदेश में नहीं आता।
    • Detention: Time और Left At भरते ही अपने-आप भर जाता है। ऊपर से बदल सकते हैं।
    • TMR No.: My profile के CMS ID / TMR No. से आता है।
    • ज़रूरी बॉक्स खाली हो तो संदेश में ___ दिखता है। आपके चुनाव से छिपे बॉक्स का मान संदेश में नहीं लिया जाता; ऐसे मान एक छोटे नोट में दिखते हैं।

    8. कॉपी, भेजना, सेव

    • Copy message – WhatsApp या SMS में पेस्ट करें।
    • Send message with evidence – फ़ोन की शेयर सूची खुलती है; WhatsApp और ग्रुप चुनें। जाँचें कि टेक्स्ट फ़ाइलों के साथ गया। शेयरिंग न चले तो कॉपी करके पेस्ट करें।
    • Send message only – टेक्स्ट तैयार करके WhatsApp या शेयर सूची खोलता है।
    • Download PDF Report – संदेश को PDF में सेव करता है। ✍ Sign pad आपका हस्ताक्षर जोड़ता है।
    • Save for later – संदेश फ़ोन में रखता है (सिग्नल न हो तब काम का)। फ़ोटो सेव नहीं होते।
    • ➕ Follow-up message (keep train details) – ट्रेन, लोको, सेक्शन, स्टेशन भरकर दूसरा सीनारियो खोलता है।
    • Follow-up: Train Ready / Started (अंतिम श्रेणी) – ट्रेन को अटेंड कर फिट करने के बाद कंट्रोल को दूसरा संदेश: रेडी समय, रवाना होने का समय (या नहीं), और किसके लिए रुकी (लाइन क्लियर, क्रॉसिंग, प्रेसिडेंस, पाथ)। अगले स्टेशन के लिए “On arrival at ___ … attended and reset” रूप भी है।
    • दूसरी जगहों पर फिर रुकना: Pressure Drop, Brake Binding और Flat Tyre में हर जगह के लिए ➕ Add stop दबाएँ और वहाँ गए मिनट लिखें। कुल डिटेंशन अपने-आप जुड़ जाता है (“Total det +13 mins”).
    • Where did it stop? रुकने वाले ज़्यादातर सीनारियो में ट्रेन और समय के तुरंत बाद यह आता है: मिड-सेक्शन, स्टेशन से गुज़रते हुए, प्लेटफ़ॉर्म पर आते / खड़े / चलते समय (शेड्यूल्ड या नॉन-शेड्यूल्ड स्टॉप, PF नंबर सहित), या स्टेशन यार्ड में। आपके चुनाव के अनुसार नीचे के बॉक्स और संदेश के शब्द बदलते हैं।
    • Informed to: ACP, C&W, OHE, इंजीनियरिंग, मेडिकल और सिक्योरिटी संदेशों में आम तौर पर सूचित किए जाने वाले कार्यालय पहले से चुने रहते हैं (जैसे ACP: Security, TXR, Commercial Control)। जाँचें और ज़रूरत हो तो जोड़ें या हटाएँ।
    • ⚠ Report wrong wording in this message – संदेश और “क्या गलत है” की खाली जगह के साथ शेयर सूची खोलता है; ऐप देखने वाले को भेजें।

    9. Saved messages और Reuse

    Saved messages में आपके सेव किए संदेश हैं। ♻ Reuse a recent message आपके कॉपी, भेजे या सेव किए आख़िरी 20 संदेश रखता है। ♻ Reuse वही सीनारियो सारे विवरण के साथ खोलता है; केवल जो बदला है वह बदलें। Reuse, clear times रुकने का समय, रवानगी का समय और डिटेंशन भी साफ़ करता है। इस अपडेट से पहले सेव हुए संदेश दोबारा नहीं चलते। फ़ोटो नहीं रखे जाते। Clear this list केवल हाल की सूची हटाता है।

    ⋯ More → My Library में ROM, SOB, Safety circulars, Flyleaf पृष्ठ, SOP, Ready reckoner और अपने प्रकार रखें। लिखकर, बोलकर, फ़ोटो, गैलरी या PDF से कार्ड जोड़ें। कार्ड नए पहले (या A–Z) आते हैं, फ़ोटो और पृष्ठ संख्या दिखाते हैं, और valid-until तारीख हो सकती है। कार्ड में A−/A+, Copy text, Edit, वैकल्पिक ☆ Pin, Share, Export card और Delete (9 सेकंड Undo) हैं। Pin किए बिना होम स्क्रीन पर कुछ नहीं दिखता।

    • टैब आख़िरी AS दिखाता है और बीच के दर्ज न हुए AS नंबरों के लिए पीला नोट। एक किताब में एक ही AS दो बार दर्ज नहीं हो सकता।

# Privacy

TMR Mitra works fully offline after the first install.

- All data (profile, signature, contacts, saved messages, photos, My Library, My record) is stored **only in your own phone's browser storage**.
- There is no server, no login, no analytics and no tracking. The app makes no requests to other websites.
- A message or file leaves the phone only when **you** tap WhatsApp, SMS, Share, Copy or Export.
- Backup files are created by you and kept where you save them. Keep them safe, because they can contain names, phone numbers and photos.
- Clearing the browser's site data, uninstalling the app or changing the phone removes the data. Use Backup regularly.

TMR Mitra is **not an official Railway tool**. It only drafts wording. Rule books and Control's orders come first.
